V1.5.1 root-cause correction - saved RAW FUSED:
- V1.5.0 fused SHORT/LONG RAW per CFA site but discarded physical color provenance before demosaic. A clipped LONG phase and differently validated SHORT phases could therefore become an invalid R/G/B relationship and WB/color conversion could amplify it into broad magenta.
- V1.5.1 preserves physical evidence beside the fused CFA in an RGBA16F carrier:
  R = fused scene-linear CFA
  G = physical color trust (valid LONG or validated aligned SHORT)
  B = physical LONG clipping risk
  A = SHORT ownership
- Highlight color authority is evaluated per exact 2x2 Bayer quad, not by independent RGB brightness.
- A fully physically proven bright color is unchanged, even when very bright.
- If physical clipping exists and any required CFA phase lacks validated evidence, the quad becomes color-incomplete and only unprovable opponent chroma is driven toward neutral while stable green/majority-derived brightness is retained.
- The trust decision is expanded by one packed Bayer quad around clipping boundaries to prevent one-pixel pink/cyan fringes. RGB itself is not blurred and no neighboring hue is borrowed.
- True-photo boundary sampling mirrors by an even CFA displacement so Bayer phase is preserved; interior tile boundaries continue to use real supplied halo data.
- Lens shading remains in the existing fusion domain; completion occurs after the LSC/WB-balanced reconstruction and before the Camera2 color matrix.
- There is NO generic smoothstep(0.70,0.99,rgb) white-desaturation rule.

V1.5.1 root-cause correction - live HDR FUSED:
- NO local tone mapping is added anywhere.
- LONG remains the body/appearance owner and existing live SHORT highlight admission/recovery remains intact.
- V1.5.0's single global shoulder compressed multi-stop recovered radiance too aggressively near display white, making HDR FUSED look nearly like clipped LONG despite usable SHORT evidence.
