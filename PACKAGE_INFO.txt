V1.4.8 correction:
- V1.4.7 HDR fusion/highlight reconstruction remains the golden baseline and is not redesigned.
- Post-fusion appearance lift is monotonic and hue-preserving. It primarily lifts darker lower/mid tones, barely moves deep shadows, gives smaller lift to already-brighter lower midtones, and converges to the V1.4.7 output through recovered highlights.
- The brightness lift is applied only after V1.4.7 adaptive HDR reconstruction in both the live GLES shader and saved JPEG fusion.
- Source-supported color reconstruction is applied after fused luminance is final: SHORT supplies exposure-safe color when it has usable signal, strong source saturation is preserved, low-amplitude chroma is gain-aware so it cannot turn into bright orange/pink/green specks, and FUSED encoded chroma is capped by the stronger actual source frame.
- The color fix is content-agnostic: there is no skin/white/foliage/device classifier, no universal sensor color matrix, no extra GPU sampling/pass, and no per-pixel Java allocation.
- AUTO SHORT headroom is capability/aperture derived, not device-ID specific. f/2 and slower retain the proven 3-EV baseline; materially wider apertures gain physically-derived target headroom from the 1/N^2 light-gathering relationship, capped at 4.25 EV.
- Under known 50/60-Hz lighting, only materially wide-aperture cases may select a closer power-of-two submultiple of the flicker period for SHORT. Ordinary apertures and unknown/PWM retain the conservative V1.4.7 same-integration behavior.
- Exposure/ISO choices remain bounded by each selected camera's actual Camera2 ranges. Measured LENS_APERTURE is preferred; advertised available apertures are the fallback.
- No Xiaomi/Motorola IDs, focal-length tables, SoC/GPU tiers, extra GPU passes, extra textures/framebuffers, neighborhood sampling, or full-frame float CPU allocations are introduced.
