V1.4.17 architecture:
- HDR image fusion remains exactly one current SHORT + one current LONG. Prior pairs provide only controller/reliability hysteresis; they never contribute image pixels.
- LONG remains the master/base image and owns all shadows, midtones, normal color and every pixel outside the highlight-recovery mask.
- System/HAL AE remains bootstrap-only. It is not the live brightness authority and no fixed office-derived +EV correction exists.
- AUTO LONG now meters the robust P25/P35/P50 scene body. Exposure-invariant P90/P50 and P98/P50 shape estimates whether a meaningful bright tail exists; that tail is assigned to SHORT rather than allowed to darken LONG.
- The scene-body target varies with histogram shape and inferred scene demand. High-demand genuine low-light scenes lower the target, while a broad bright-tail scene raises it. This prevents the V1.4.16 fixed 0.045-linear office target from becoming a global brightness lock.
- LONG convergence requires two agreeing samples and is bounded to 0.18EV per update. Brightness remains -5.0..+2.0EV and biases the learned LONG appearance target rather than directly changing bracket width.
- SHORT remains independent highlight insurance. Bracket widening still requires stable meaningful clipping evidence and is blocked when SHORT is broadly unreliable.
- Live fusion is now masked highlight recovery: SHORT has no authority outside LONG's highlight-need mask.
- The live 32x24 reliability field is spatial. R stores SHORT luminance/detail trust; G stores SHORT chroma trust. It is bilinear filtered, so one unstable lamp cannot globally disable a stable recoverable window.
- Luminance and color trust are separate. SHORT may contribute useful highlight brightness/detail while questionable SHORT chroma is rejected and LONG chromaticity is retained.
- Neutral clipped LONG regions have an additional anti-pink color lock. A white lamp/reflection may recover highlight structure without importing pink/orange processed-ISP tint.
- A clipped/low-signal/unstable SHORT region falls back locally to LONG. Clean stable clipping remains preferable to corrupted recovery.
- Recovered SHORT highlights receive a wider useful display range (recovery knee 0.72, white anchor 0.90, ceiling 0.995) only inside the mask, so SHORT detail is not squeezed into near-white while ordinary LONG mids remain untouched.
- Saved JPEG fusion mirrors the LONG-base mask and separates luminance/detail trust from chroma trust. It does not apply global Brightness gain.
- Exposure-generation matching, temporal anti-pulsation, known/unknown flicker guards, AUTO/MANUAL adaptive SHORT policy, frozen still pair, FOV/cadence, orientation/DNG, sRGB, edge/noise ownership and stable signing are inherited from successful V1.4.16.
