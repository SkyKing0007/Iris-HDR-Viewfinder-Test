V1.4.15 architecture:
- Initial clean AE is bootstrap-only. After first anchor, periodic 5-second AE takeover/remeter is removed.
- Live SHORT/LONG burst remains active continuously; 32x24 displayed-pair scene statistics are sampled at low duty to update exposure without replacing the repeating HDR request.
- LONG owns natural scene appearance and user Brightness intent (-5.0..+2.0 EV).
- AUTO SHORT independently adapts only as dark as needed for meaningful highlight recovery, normally 3 EV and bounded to 5 EV.
- Tiny clipped areas below the meaningful-area budget are allowed to remain clipped rather than forcing excessively dark/noisy SHORT.
- MANUAL SAFE retains user LONG/brightness intent but uses the same adaptive SHORT logic, with slightly more headroom and a 6 EV ceiling.
- SHORT quality guard uses dark-pixel fraction, overlap sample count, and overlap error to avoid blotchy over-normalization.
- Capture metadata reports the new LONG_APPEARANCE_SHORT_ADAPTIVE ownership instead of the retired whole-pair label.
- Live and saved fusion derive per-channel SHORT-to-LONG overlap calibration before recovery.
- LONG remains complete RGB/color owner wherever valid. SHORT only recovers genuinely clipped channels when normalized SHORT proves extra radiance and valid channels agree.
- V1.4.7 full-RGB one-bright-channel handoff is removed to address peach/orange skin and colored-white reflective contamination without global desaturation/chroma filtering.
- Highlight tone mapping preserves LONG mids/appearance and compresses only the upper/recovered range.
- 50/60-Hz, unknown/PWM, forced-60 cadence, FOV, orientation, DNG, frozen capture-set and logging protections remain.
