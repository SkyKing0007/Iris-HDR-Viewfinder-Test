V2.39 CORRECTION CONTRACT

2. Luma and chroma cleanup are separate. In statistically noise-like smooth interiors, luma cleanup is capped at 0.52 and chroma cleanup at 0.92. Coherent 1-2px luminance structure suppresses both; bilateral luma/chroma range compatibility prevents averaging across real colored or luminance edges.
3. The new ordinary-noise cleanup is disabled at the physical saturation center so V2.38 common-quad/highlight and saturation-transition chroma ownership remains unchanged. The output alpha is copied from the center carrier exactly; cleanup may not advertise artificially lower physical sigma to downstream fusion.
4. V2.39 removes the retired V2.38 isolated 5-sigma 1.5px path from coarse `longEffectiveLossAt`. Medium/broad connected HDR loss remains noise-normalized and unchanged in purpose.
5. A new independent native-resolution microdetail owner evaluates multiple center-to-ring and ring-to-ring SHORT/LONG gradients. SHORT gradients must be above physical difference noise and materially stronger than LONG; both center and ring support are required so a single noisy center pixel cannot trigger.
6. Direct microdetail authority requires complete 4x4 SHORT source bounds, direct cycle-validated local-flow confidence, registration-neighborhood confidence, low-frequency SHORT/LONG same-scene support, valid SHORT signal/headroom and a low-residual-motion barrier.
