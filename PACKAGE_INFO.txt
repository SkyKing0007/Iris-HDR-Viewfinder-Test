- If current SHORT remains signal-safe/unclipped, clipped-core detail authority reaches 1.0; slower luma history may shape only the shoulder, never attenuate the true core.
- The outer highlight shoulder remains a soft blend to prevent hard seams.
- Local 32x24 luma trust uses graded attack/release for the soft shoulder instead of one-sample binary reset; true clipped-core luma/detail is driven by current SHORT usability and cannot blink from the 5-Hz history state.

- At shutter time the current two-channel live trust field is frozen and propagated through CameraController -> CaptureSetSaver -> JpegFusion.
- Full-resolution JPEG fusion bilinearly samples the frozen field for shoulder/chroma confidence and uses the same clipped-core/soft-shoulder contract; the captured SHORT itself owns clipped-core luma permission so stale preview history cannot suppress recoverable still detail.
- Questionable SHORT color may never veto good SHORT detail; LONG chromaticity is retained at SHORT-derived recovered luminance when chroma trust is lower.
- Neutral clipped LONG highlights retain anti-pink/anti-orange protection.
- If SHORT is itself clipped/dark/persistently unreliable, recovery falls back locally to LONG.
- No prior preview frame contributes image pixels; history affects only the small reliability field.
