V1.4.23 information-gain SHORT search:

- A darker SHORT is a temporary probe, not an automatically accepted new tier.
- The probe is accepted only when localized LONG-damaged regions gain measurable usable recovery information.
- A no-gain probe rolls back once to the previously accepted tier and locks further darkening until a material LONG-scene change.
- A phase-sensitive fast SHORT with uncorrectable flicker evidence is rejected even if apparent highlight headroom increases.
- Stable scenes may not oscillate repeatedly between adjacent SHORT tiers.
- The achieved physical bracket remains hard-bounded after shutter quantization.

- The field uses same-row SHORT/LONG overlap evidence and separates luma trust from chroma trust.
- Fast/PWM-risk SHORT is normalized only where current-pair evidence is sufficient and consistent; unsafe regions fail closed from recovery.

- FrameMeta carries the fast-SHORT guard with the exact exposure-generation pair into still capture.

- This avoids restoring full-frame Java HDR processing or huge extra full-resolution GPU history allocations.
