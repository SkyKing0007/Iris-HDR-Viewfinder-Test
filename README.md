## V1.5.4 corrections

### Saved RAW HDR artifact correction

V1.5.3 proved that SHORT highlight recovery works, but the plant-rack test exposed white paint/strokes plus small green/magenta/orange fragments generated by FUSED even though neither source exposure contained them.

V1.5.4 preserves the successful whole-Bayer-quad exposure ownership and unconditional clipped-LONG -> SHORT rule, while separating **exposure ownership** from **physical chroma trust**:

- a physically clipped LONG quad can remain 100% SHORT-owned;
- hard SHORT takeover no longer promotes weak support/alignment to perfect R/G1/G2/B trust;
- incomplete SHORT chroma evidence is handled conservatively without ever returning clipped LONG;
- demosaic trust changes continuously rather than switching at the old support 0.30 / 1.10 / 1.50 or `highOrderTrust=0.95` boundaries;
- clipped-highlight neutralization now targets the reconstructed green/luminance anchor `vec3(g)` instead of `max(g, medianRGB)`, so uncertainty may remove opponent chroma but may **not create extra luminance or white paint**.

The broad-pink/ceiling-magenta protection remains structurally preserved, as do whole-quad source coherence and the no-peach/orange synthetic-fill contract.

### Fast but stable AUTO HDR response

Steady-state behavior is still the proven conservative controller: 0.18 EV maximum normal update, two confirming samples, and the existing hysteresis.

A new scene-cut path activates only when the robust body error is at least 0.90 EV. A true scene change can apply a bounded correction of up to 4 EV in the current stats update and bypass the normal pair-update throttle once. If the current matched SHORT proves unresolved highlight clipping, SHORT receives one immediate extra stop of headroom. After that jump the controller returns to the conservative path.

This is intentionally designed to improve dark<->bright response without creating steady-state flicker, pumping, or highlight clipping.

### HDR MANUAL SAFE SHORT control

The old implementation treated the SHORT slider as a ceiling and also imposed a hidden ~3.25 EV minimum adaptive bracket, so moving SHORT could be ignored.

V1.5.4 starts each manual slider change exactly at the user-requested SHORT/LONG bracket. Measured highlight safety may subsequently make SHORT darker/shorter if needed, but it may not impose hidden headroom before the user's setting is displayed. The existing user-requested SHORT ceiling remains in force, so safety can never make SHORT longer than requested.

### Brightness range

Brightness is now exactly **-16.0 EV through +1.0 EV**, in 0.1-EV steps, across:

- MainActivity UI/state;
- CameraController clamping/exposure intent;
- CaptureSetSaver metadata.

No post-fusion GL brightness gain is added; Brightness remains exposure intent.
